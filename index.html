HOW IT WORKS

Drag any of the three knurled knobs to turn that leveling screw. The bubble runs toward the screw you raise. Centre it inside the dashed ring and hold it there until the ring closes — a bubble that only touches centre on its way past hasn't been levelled.

Field trick that actually works: turn two knobs at once, in opposite directions — the bubble then moves on one axis only, so you can kill one error at a time instead of chasing it in circles.
